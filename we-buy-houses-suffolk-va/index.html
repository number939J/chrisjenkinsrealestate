Selling Your Suffolk House for Cash

Suffolk is the largest city in Virginia by land area, at 430 square miles. The north has suburban neighborhoods close to the rest of Hampton Roads, while the south is rural. A house on a half-acre in a subdivision and a farmhouse on ten acres are different markets.

I have not published Suffolk neighborhood statistics on this site yet, so I will not quote a median. Send your address and I will pull comparable closed sales from REIN MLS and show you the numbers. Rural properties can have few close comparables, and I will tell you when that makes pricing harder.

Cash Sale vs. Listing With an Agent in Suffolk

PriceA cash offer usually comes in below what a repaired, well-marketed home can fetch on the open market. You are paying for speed, certainty and convenience.
SpeedA cash buyer does not wait on mortgage approval or a lender appraisal. A listing depends on how fast the right buyer and their financing line up.
Repairs and showingsSell as-is with no repairs, staging or open houses, versus preparing and showing the home when you list.
CertaintyFewer contingencies means fewer ways for the deal to fall through, though every sale still depends on clear title and a signed contract.
CostsNo listing commission on a direct sale, but closing costs and taxes still apply. Virginia’s state recordation tax is 25 cents per $100 of the price (Va. Code § 58.1-801), and other fees can apply. Your settlement agent itemizes all of it.

If your home is in good shape and you are not in a hurry, listing with an agent will often net you more, and I will tell you that straight. I am a full-time REALTOR®, so I can help you either way.

Common Questions

Suffolk Cash Sale Questions, Answered

Do you buy Suffolk houses in any condition?
I look at houses in many conditions, including ones that need major work. Tell me what you know and I will give an honest answer.
How do you decide what to offer on a Suffolk house?
I pull closed sales near your address from REIN MLS, subtract the repairs and updates the house needs and the cost and risk of holding and reselling it, and show you the math. A cash offer is generally below what a fully prepared house could get on the open market.
Do you buy rural property or land with a house?
I look at them case by case. Acreage, wells, septic and access all affect value. Send the address and details and I will say honestly whether it fits.
How do you price when there are few comparable sales?
I widen the search area and look at lot size, age and condition, and I tell you plainly where the offer rests on fewer comparables.
Who can sell an inherited Suffolk property?
Generally the executor or administrator, after qualifying with the circuit court clerk. With several heirs, talk to a Virginia attorney first.
Can I sell before foreclosure?
In most cases you can sell until the foreclosure sale takes place. Read this guide.
Do I pay a Realtor commission if I sell to you in Suffolk?
On a direct sale there is no listing commission. Closing costs and taxes still apply, and I will go over them with you before you decide. I will also tell you honestly what listing would likely net you.
How do I know this is not a scam?
Ask who the actual buyer is, ask for proof of funds, get the offer in writing, close through a title company or attorney and check any license with the Virginia DPOR.
