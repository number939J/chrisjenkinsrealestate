Cash Offer vs. Listing Your House in Hampton Roads: What You Actually Keep

A cash offer is almost always lower than a list price. The honest question is whether it is lower than what you would actually keep after listing. Here is how to compare.

By Chris Jenkins · Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group · Updated November 21, 2026

The short answer

Listing usually brings the highest price but adds repairs, showings, commission, carrying costs and a buyer who may not close. A direct sale usually brings a lower price with fewer costs and more certainty. Compare net proceeds and time, not just the headline number.

The Number That Matters Is What You Keep

A list price and a cash offer are not comparable. Compare what lands in your bank account after everything is paid, and when. Use this worksheet for both paths:

  • Sale price you expect, based on closed sales nearby, not asking prices.
  • Minus repairs and prep a listing needs to sell at that price.
  • Minus agent commission and seller closing costs, which a direct sale generally avoids.
  • Minus carrying costs (mortgage, taxes, insurance, utilities) for the weeks or months to sell and close.
  • Minus concessions a buyer may ask for after the inspection or appraisal.
  • Minus the risk of a deal falling through and starting over.

Recordation tax and other transfer costs fall under the Virginia Recordation Tax Act; your closing agent itemizes them either way.

When Listing Usually Wins

  • The house is updated and well kept, so there is little to discount.
  • You are not in a hurry and can wait for the right buyer.
  • The house is in a high-demand neighborhood with a large buyer pool.

For example, REIN data on this site shows the median Virginia Beach home took 15 days to sell, per the days-to-sell data. That is for the typical house, not yours.

When a Direct Sale Usually Wins

  • The house needs significant repairs you do not want to pay for.
  • You have a hard date: a PCS report date, an estate deadline or a foreclosure timeline.
  • A vacant or damaged house is costing you every month.
  • You want a single closing date and no showings or negotiations.

Cash Sale vs. Listing With an Agent in Hampton Roads

PriceA cash offer usually comes in below what a repaired, well-marketed home can fetch on the open market. You are paying for speed, certainty and convenience.
SpeedA cash buyer does not wait on mortgage approval or a lender appraisal. A listing depends on how fast the right buyer and their financing line up.
Repairs and showingsSell as-is with no repairs, staging or open houses, versus preparing and showing the home when you list.
CertaintyFewer contingencies means fewer ways for the deal to fall through, though every sale still depends on clear title and a signed contract.
CostsNo listing commission on a direct sale, but closing costs and taxes still apply. Virginia’s state recordation tax is 25 cents per $100 of the price (Va. Code § 58.1-801), and other fees can apply. Your settlement agent itemizes all of it.

If your home is in good shape and you are not in a hurry, listing with an agent will often net you more, and I will tell you that straight. I am a full-time REALTOR®, so I can help you either way.

Common Questions

Hampton Roads Cash Sale Questions, Answered

Is a cash offer always lower than listing?
Usually, because the buyer takes on repairs, holding costs and risk. What matters is whether it is lower than what you would keep after listing.
Do I save the commission on a direct sale?
There is no listing commission on a direct sale. Other closing costs still apply.
Which is faster?
A direct sale can close on your timeline once title is clear. A listing depends on finding a buyer and their financing.
What can go wrong with a listing?
Inspection repairs, appraisal gaps and financing problems can delay or cancel a sale.
Can I do both?
You can get a cash offer first and list if the numbers favor it. There is no obligation.
How do I know the list price is realistic?
Use closed sales, not asking prices. I will show you the comparable sales.
Does a cash offer have taxes or fees?
Closing costs and transfer taxes apply. The title company or attorney itemizes them.
Is this financial or tax advice?
No. Talk to a tax advisor about gains and deductions.
