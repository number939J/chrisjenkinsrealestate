Is Chesapeake or Virginia Beach cheaper to buy a home in?

Chesapeake vs Virginia Beach home prices from REIN MLS closed sales: median price, price per square foot, days on market and negotiation room, compared by Chris Jenkins.

By Chris Jenkins · Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group · Updated November 15, 2026

Home/Questions/Is Chesapeake or Virginia Beach cheaper to buy a home in?
Short answer

Based on closed sales from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, Virginia Beach had the lower median sold price: Chesapeake $420,000 versus Virginia Beach $405,000. Price per square foot was $219 in Chesapeake and $240 in Virginia Beach.

Both cities are large and varied, so the answer depends on which part of each you are comparing. Here are the city-wide figures from REIN MLS closed residential sales from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026.
