What are the most affordable neighborhoods in Virginia Beach, VA?

Virginia Beach VA neighborhood ranking from REIN MLS closed sales: lowest median price, with sales counts and links to each guide.

By Chris Jenkins · Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group · Updated November 15, 2026

Home/Questions/What are the most affordable neighborhoods in Virginia Beach, VA?
Short answer

Among the 25 Virginia Beach neighborhoods with guides on this site (REIN MLS sales, November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026), Campus East had the lowest median sold price at $250,000, followed by Ridgely Manor ($302,100) and Kempsville Lake ($315,000). The Virginia Beach city-wide median was $405,000.

This list covers the 25 Virginia Beach neighborhoods that have guides on this site, based on closed sales in REIN MLS from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026. It is not every neighborhood, and neighborhoods follow MLS subdivision names. The ranking uses the median, so a few unusual sales do not decide it.

How to Use This List

A neighborhood's median describes the typical sale, not any one home. Compare the homes you are considering against recent sales on the same street, and check flood zone, association fees and noise zones for the specific address. Use the compare tool to put two neighborhoods side by side.
