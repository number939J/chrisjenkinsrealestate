Relocating to Hampton Roads

What should I do before moving to Hampton Roads?

A relocation checklist for moving to Hampton Roads: commute testing, flood and noise checks, school zone verification and a simple plan for your first visit.

By Chris Jenkins · Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group · Updated November 15, 2026

Home/Questions/What should I do before moving to Hampton Roads?
Short answer

Spend most of a visit in the neighborhoods, not just tourist areas. Test your commute at real hours, check flood and military noise exposure, confirm school zones directly with the school district, and ask for video tours before you travel.

Relocation is a big, emotional move. Take the stress out of it with a clear plan.

Before You Fly In

  1. Get pre-approved so you know your real budget.
  2. Build a short list using the compare tool and our neighborhood guides.
  3. Tour by video to cut the list down before you travel.

On Your Visit

  1. Drive your commute at the hours you will drive it.
  2. Visit at different times of day to see traffic, noise and activity.
  3. Check flood exposure for each address: flood insurance basics.
  4. Verify school zones directly with the school district; do not rely on a listing.

Ask about taxes, insurance and HOA costs so you compare total monthly cost, not only price. The buyer checklist covers the rest.
