Hampton Roads Real Estate Questions, Answered

Short, sourced answers to the questions buyers and sellers ask most, built on closed-sale data from REIN MLS. From Chris Jenkins, Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group.

Home/Questions

Hampton Roads Comparisons

  • Is Chesapeake or Virginia Beach cheaper to buy a home in?
    Chesapeake $420,000 vs Virginia Beach $405,000 median sold price.
  • Do homes in Chesapeake and Virginia Beach sell for more than the asking price?
    37% of Chesapeake and 36% of Virginia Beach sales closed above list.

Chesapeake Selling Questions

  • How long does it take to sell a house in Chesapeake, VA?
    Median 18 days on market; 44% sold within 14 days.

Virginia Beach Selling Questions

  • How long does it take to sell a house in Virginia Beach, VA?
    Median 15 days on market; 49% sold within 14 days.

Buying in Hampton Roads

  • Do I need flood insurance when buying a home in Hampton Roads?
    Depends on the address: check the FEMA flood map and get a quote before your inspection period ends.
  • How do I choose a home based on my commute to a base or shipyard?
    Drive the real route at real hours, including water crossings, and time the trip to your gate or site before you offer.
  • What is the military noise zone disclosure when buying a home in Hampton Roads?
    Sellers must disclose a military air installation noise or accident potential zone; buyers should also check the city map.
  • What is an HOA resale certificate in Virginia, and can I cancel after reading it?
    It is the association's disclosure packet; buyers get a limited right to cancel, and the association has 14 days to deliver it.

Selling in Hampton Roads

  • How should I price my home to sell in Hampton Roads?
    Price from recent closed sales of similar nearby homes, not asking prices or what you owe, and aim to attract buyers in week one.

Chesapeake Home Prices

  • What is the median home price in Chesapeake, VA?
    $420,000 median sold price across 7,291 closed sales; middle half $316,000 to $565,000.

Virginia Beach Home Prices

  • What is the median home price in Virginia Beach, VA?
    $405,000 median sold price across 12,010 closed sales; middle half $320,000 to $574,000.

Military and VA Buyers

  • What mistakes do military buyers make when buying a home in Hampton Roads?
    Choosing by commute alone, skipping flood and noise-zone checks, ignoring BAH math, and not planning for resale or renting.
  • What does a VA buyer need to know about appraisals and inspections?
    The VA appraisal checks value and minimum property requirements. It is not an inspection, so get your own.

Hampton Roads Neighborhoods

  • Which Chesapeake and Virginia Beach neighborhoods have the most home sales?
    Virginia Beach: Green Run (324), Ocean Lakes (289), Princess Anne Plaza (280). Chesapeake: South Norfolk (217), Riverwalk (188), Norfolk Highlands (178).

Chesapeake Neighborhoods

  • What are the most affordable neighborhoods in Chesapeake, VA?
    Georgetown Colony at $270,700, then Greenbrier and Riverwalk.
  • What are the most expensive neighborhoods in Chesapeake, VA?
    Stoney Creek at $744,520, then Hickory and Pleasant Grove.
  • Which Chesapeake, VA neighborhoods have the newest homes?
    Retreat at Edinburgh, then Jolliff Landing at Western Branch and Stoney Creek: median year built 2025.

Virginia Beach Neighborhoods

  • What are the most affordable neighborhoods in Virginia Beach, VA?
    Campus East at $250,000, then Ridgely Manor and Kempsville Lake.
  • What are the most expensive neighborhoods in Virginia Beach, VA?
    Ashville Park at $1,218,153, then North End Virginia Beach and Sandbridge Beach.
  • Which Virginia Beach, VA neighborhoods have the newest homes?
    Ashville Park, then Ridgely Manor and Sandbridge Beach: median year built 2024.

Relocating to Hampton Roads

  • What should I do before moving to Hampton Roads?
    Test your commute, check flood and noise exposure, confirm school zones with the district, and tour by video first.
