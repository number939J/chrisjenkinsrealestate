Chesapeake Home Prices

What is the median home price in Chesapeake, VA?

Chesapeake VA median home price is $420,000 from 7,291 closed REIN MLS sales, with a breakdown by area and price trend from Chris Jenkins.

By Chris Jenkins · Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group · Updated November 15, 2026

Home/Questions/What is the median home price in Chesapeake, VA?
Short answer

The median sold price in Chesapeake, VA was $420,000 across 7,291 closed sales from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, according to REIN MLS. The middle half of sales closed between $316,000 and $565,000.

Chesapeake's median sold price was $420,000 for the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, based on 7,291 closed residential sales recorded in REIN MLS. Closed sales show what buyers actually paid, not asking prices. The price per square foot was about $219 at the median.

Is the Price Rising or Falling?

Splitting the period in two, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months was $434,900 (3,690 sales) against $409,000 (3,601 sales) in the 12 months before, which is 6.3% higher. One city-wide number can hide big differences between parts of Chesapeake, so here is the same data by MLS area.

What Moves the Price

Location, size, age, condition, lot and whether a home is detached or attached drive most of the spread. Check the specific street with recent comparable sales rather than relying on the Chesapeake median.
