Selling Your Chesapeake House for Cash

Chesapeake is not one market. REIN MLS records show 7,291 closed residential sales in Chesapeake between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026. The citywide median sold price was $420,000, the median home took 18 days to sell, and the median price per square foot was $219.

Neighborhoods vary widely. The median sold price in Riverwalk was $281,750 (0.7x the citywide median), while in Hickory it was $717,500 (1.7x). A citywide average hides all of that, which is why I price every cash offer from sales near your address and show you the numbers.

Probate, Inherited Homes and Foreclosure in Chesapeake

Virginia probate is handled by the circuit court clerk in the city or county where the person lived, so for a Chesapeake resident that is the Chesapeake Circuit Court clerk’s office. Read how to sell an inherited house in Hampton Roads.

If you are behind on a mortgage, Virginia foreclosures are generally non-judicial and timelines can be short. In most cases you can sell until the foreclosure sale takes place. Read how to sell before a foreclosure sale, and talk to a Virginia attorney or housing counselor right away.

Selling in a Chesapeake HOA Community

Many newer Chesapeake neighborhoods have homeowners associations, and Virginia has its own disclosure rules for HOA sales. Read how the HOA resale certificate works. If you have unpaid dues or open violations, tell me up front so we can plan for them.

Cash Sale vs. Listing With an Agent in Chesapeake

PriceA cash offer usually comes in below what a repaired, well-marketed home can fetch on the open market. You are paying for speed, certainty and convenience.
SpeedA cash buyer does not wait on mortgage approval or a lender appraisal. A listing depends on how fast the right buyer and their financing line up.
Repairs and showingsSell as-is with no repairs, staging or open houses, versus preparing and showing the home when you list.
CertaintyFewer contingencies means fewer ways for the deal to fall through, though every sale still depends on clear title and a signed contract.
CostsNo listing commission on a direct sale, but closing costs and taxes still apply. Virginia’s state recordation tax is 25 cents per $100 of the price (Va. Code § 58.1-801), and other fees can apply. Your settlement agent itemizes all of it.

If your home is in good shape and you are not in a hurry, listing with an agent will often net you more, and I will tell you that straight. I am a full-time REALTOR®, so I can help you either way.

Common Questions

Chesapeake Cash Sale Questions, Answered

Do you buy Chesapeake houses in any condition?
I look at houses in many conditions, including ones that need major repairs. Tell me what you know about the roof, systems, water or structural issues and I will give you an honest answer.
How do you price a cash offer in Chesapeake?
I start from closed sales near your address, subtract the repairs and updates needed and the cost and risk of holding and reselling the house, and show you the math. Chesapeake prices vary a lot by neighborhood, from about 0.7x to 1.8x the citywide median, so nearby sales matter more than the citywide figure.
I live in South Norfolk, Greenbrier or Plymouth Park. Do you buy there?
Yes, I look at houses in all Chesapeake neighborhoods. These are among the older, lower-priced areas, and a cash sale often fits houses that need updating. See the data for South Norfolk, Greenbrier and Plymouth Park.
How long would it take to sell if I listed?
REIN shows the median Chesapeake home took 18 days to sell over the last 24 months, but newer neighborhoods like Retreat at Edinburgh had a median of 61 days. Read days to sell a house in Chesapeake.
I have an HOA. Does that change anything?
Virginia has its own HOA resale disclosure rules, and unpaid dues or violations should be handled in the sale. Read this explainer and tell me about your association.
Can I sell my Chesapeake house before foreclosure?
In most cases you can sell until the foreclosure sale takes place, with the proceeds paying off the lender. Timelines can be short. Read this guide and talk with a Virginia attorney or housing counselor.
Who can sell an inherited Chesapeake house?
Generally the executor or administrator, after qualifying with the Chesapeake Circuit Court clerk. A Virginia attorney can confirm the steps for your estate.
Do I pay a Realtor commission if I sell to you?
On a direct sale there is no listing commission. Closing costs and taxes still apply, and I will go over them before you decide. I will also tell you honestly what listing would likely net you.
