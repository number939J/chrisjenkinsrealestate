VA Appraisal vs. Home Inspection

What a VA appraisal checks, what it does not, and what to ask your lender before you write an offer. Read the full answer. Watch on YouTube.

Read the transcript

VA buyers, listen up. The appraisal is not your home inspection. The VA appraisal checks two things. That the value supports the price, and that the home meets VA minimum property requirements. It doesn't tell you what shape the roof, the H V A C, or the plumbing is in. That's a home inspection, and you still want one. Before you write an offer, ask your lender what happens if the appraisal comes in low, and what the seller can pay. I'm Chris Jenkins. Military buyer in Hampton Roads? Send me your questions.
