Buying in Sandbridge, Virginia Beach: Taxes, Flood Zones, Rentals

Buying in Sandbridge, Virginia Beach: recent REIN MLS sales, the Sandbridge SSD tax rate, VE flood zones and short-term rental permits, with Chris Jenkins.

Short answer: buying in Sandbridge Beach, the beach community in southeastern Virginia Beach, means checking three things that most inland purchases never raise: a special service district (SSD) tax rate, the address's flood zone (including possible VE coastal zones), and whether the home is eligible for a short-term rental permit if you plan to rent it out. Recent sales are also well above the citywide median. Between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026, 140 homes closed in Sandbridge at a median of $1,032,500, according to REIN MLS records summarized on our Sandbridge Beach neighborhood guide. Chris Jenkins, a REALTOR® with Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group, walks buyers through each of these checks before they make an offer.

What Have Sandbridge Homes Sold For Recently?

Closed sales tell you what buyers actually paid. Here are the main figures from the same 24-month REIN MLS window:

  • Median sold price: $1,032,500, compared with a Virginia Beach citywide median of $405,000.
  • Middle half of sales: $515,000 to $1,511,250, so the range is wide.
  • Median price per square foot: $399, versus $240 citywide.
  • Median days on market: 56, versus 15 citywide.
  • Home mix: about 56% detached homes and 44% townhomes or condos, with a median size of about 2,260 square feet and a median year built of 2004.

The longer marketing time stands out. Many Sandbridge homes are higher-priced and more specialized than a typical suburban house, so buyers often take longer to decide. That can give a buyer more room to ask questions and complete due diligence, but it is a past pattern, not a forecast. For citywide context, see our answers on the median home price in Virginia Beach and how long homes take to sell in Virginia Beach.

What Is the Sandbridge Special Service District Tax?

A special service district is a defined area where the city applies a separate tax rate to fund specific projects or services. The Virginia Beach Real Estate Assessor's tax rates page, which presents Fiscal Year 2027 rates, lists the general real estate rate at $0.97 per $100 of assessed value and a Sandbridge SSD rate of $0.98 per $100. The same table lists other districts across the city, such as Shadowlawn and several neighborhood dredging districts.

The assessor's page gives the rates but does not explain how the SSD line combines with the general rate on a bill. Before you budget, Chris Jenkins suggests two steps:

  1. Ask the seller, the listing agent or the City Treasurer for the most recent real estate tax bill for the specific property.
  2. Ask your closing attorney or title company to confirm whether the address is inside the Sandbridge SSD and how proration will be handled at settlement.

Our Virginia Beach real estate tax guide explains assessments, due dates and appeals in more detail.

How Do Flood Zones Work in Sandbridge?

Sandbridge is a beach community, so flood zone research belongs in every purchase there. FEMA defines Zone VE as coastal areas with a 1% or greater annual chance of flooding plus an added hazard from storm waves, which works out to about a 26% chance of flooding over a 30-year mortgage. Zone AE carries the same 1% annual chance without the wave hazard designation.

The City of Virginia Beach floodplain management page says new or substantially improved homes in a Special Flood Hazard Area must have the lowest floor above the base flood elevation; in a V zone, the measurement is taken at the lowest horizontal structural member. The city notes that required freeboard varies by flood zone and location, and that repairs or improvements worth 50% or more of a building's market value must meet current floodplain rules. The city also participates in the National Flood Insurance Program.

To check a specific address, the city points to its online map layer labeled "Flood Zones – Effective Jan. 16, 2015" or a flood zone determination request through its floodplain staff at (757) 385-4621. Then ask the seller for an elevation certificate and get a flood insurance quote early. Our elevation certificate guide and the flood insurance question page cover the details.

Can You Rent Out a Sandbridge Home Short-Term?

Many buyers ask this, and the answer depends on the specific property. The Virginia Beach Planning Department defines a short-term rental as a dwelling rented for transient stays of less than 30 consecutive days. Its rules say an eligible property needs an annual zoning permit before the owner accepts reservations, the permit is issued to the registered owner, and it must match the bedroom count on file with the assessor. Eligibility depends on the property's location and on its status before the zoning rules took effect, and the department provides an online eligibility map.

The Commissioner of the Revenue adds its own steps: register annually with that office, get full Planning Department clearance before renting, then file monthly reports and remit transient occupancy taxes. Because eligibility does not automatically transfer with every sale, Chris Jenkins recommends confirming a home's status with the city's short-term rental team before you count on rental income. Treat any income projection as an estimate, not a promise.

How Does Sandbridge Compare With Nearby Virginia Beach Areas?

Our guides list several southern Virginia Beach areas near Sandbridge. Over the same 24-month window, Lagomar had a median of $942,500 with 14 median days on market, Ashville Park had a median of $1,218,153 with 11 days, and Ocean Lakes had a median of $420,000 with 12 days. You can line these up by price, size and home type with the neighborhood compare tool. If you plan to use a VA loan, run the numbers in the VA loan payment calculator, and add flood insurance and the SSD tax line to your estimate.

Common Questions

Quick Answers

What is the median home price in Sandbridge, Virginia Beach?
REIN MLS closed sales from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026 show a median of $1,032,500 across 140 Sandbridge sales, per Chris Jenkins' Sandbridge Beach guide. The middle half sold between $515,000 and $1,511,250.
What is the Sandbridge special service district tax rate?
The Virginia Beach assessor's tax rates page, presenting FY2027 rates, lists the Sandbridge SSD at $0.98 per $100 of assessed value and the general rate at $0.97. Confirm how it applies to a specific property with the Treasurer or your closing attorney.
Is Sandbridge in a flood zone?
Flood zones vary by address. Virginia Beach's flood map shows AE and VE zones, and FEMA defines VE as a coastal zone with added storm wave hazards. Check each address on the city's flood zone map or request a determination from the city.
Can I use a Sandbridge home as a short-term rental?
Only if the property is eligible. Virginia Beach requires an annual zoning permit issued to the owner, annual registration with the Commissioner of the Revenue, and monthly transient occupancy tax filings. Chris Jenkins of Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group recommends confirming eligibility with the city before buying.
