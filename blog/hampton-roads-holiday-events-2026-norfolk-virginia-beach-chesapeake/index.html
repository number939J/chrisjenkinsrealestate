Hampton Roads Holiday Events 2026: Norfolk, Beach, Chesapeake

Dates and details for 2026 holiday events in Hampton Roads: Norfolk's Grand Illumination, Virginia Beach lights and Chesapeake's parade, from Chris Jenkins.

By Chris Jenkins · Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group · Published November 21, 2026

Home/Blog/Hampton Roads Holiday Events 2026: Norfolk, Beach, Chesapeake
Quick answer

In 2026, Hampton Roads holiday highlights include Norfolk's Grand Illumination Parade on November 21, the Holiday Trail of Lights at the Virginia Aquarium through January 3, 2027, and the Chesapeake Rotary Christmas Parade on December 5. Chris Jenkins of Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group compiled these details from official event pages; confirm times before you go.

Short answer: the big public holiday events in Hampton Roads this season are the Grand Illumination Parade in Downtown Norfolk on Saturday, November 21, the Holiday Trail of Lights at the Virginia Aquarium in Virginia Beach (November 20 through January 3, 2027), and the Chesapeake Rotary Christmas Parade on December 5. Chris Jenkins, a REALTOR® with Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group, pulled the details below from official event pages as of November 21, 2026. Dates and road closures can change, so confirm before you go.

When Is the Norfolk Grand Illumination Parade in 2026?

Downtown Norfolk's 40th annual Grand Illumination Parade is scheduled for Saturday, November 21, 2026, at 7 p.m. The theme is “Ruby Radiance.” The countdown, skyline lighting and parade all begin at 7 p.m., and the event is free and runs rain or shine. Organizers say it lasts about an hour from when the first unit reaches your spot.

Plan for crowds. Local news reports organizers expect up to 80,000 spectators and about 91 parade units. Police begin closing streets around 5 p.m., and the two-mile route runs from St. Paul's Boulevard and Main Street through Granby, Freemason and Boush streets to Waterside Drive near Water Street. Street seating is not provided, though chairs are allowed.

Hampton Roads Transit is offering a $4 special-event light rail and ferry pass for parade day, with riders 17 and under free with a paid adult. Detailed closure maps are posted on norfolk.gov/raceandparade.

What Is the Holiday Trail of Lights in Virginia Beach?

The long-running drive-through lights show at the Oceanfront has ended after 31 years, according to WHRO Public Media. In its place is the Holiday Trail of Lights, a walkable display with seven themed areas on the nature trail at the Virginia Aquarium & Marine Science Center. WHRO reported timed tickets and a run from November 20 through January 3, 2027, with adult tickets listed at about $19 to $22 and children's at about $12 to $15, plus discounts for military families on Monday nights and for Aquarium members. Tickets are sold through BeachEventsVB.com.

Because the format changed, check the official ticket page for your date and time slot before you drive over. Some older listings still describe the previous Boardwalk route.

What Else Is Happening in Virginia Beach in December?

Visit Virginia Beach lists a full month of seasonal events, including:

  • Snow Globe Spectacular at Town Center, November 28 to December 23.
  • Gingerbread House Exhibit at the Atlantic Wildfowl Heritage Museum, November 23 to December 29, listed as free.
  • Holiday Boat Parade on Lynnhaven Inlet, December 12, listed as free.
  • Surf-n-Santa Five Miler, December 11–12, starting at the Convention Center and running along the Boardwalk.
  • Last Night on the Town at Town Center, December 31 into January 1, listed as free.

If you are exploring the Oceanfront area, our guide to Beach Borough covers recent home sales there.

When Is the Chesapeake Christmas Parade?

The 43rd annual Chesapeake Rotary Christmas Parade is listed for Saturday, December 5, 2026, with the Holly Jolly Jog fun run at 5:30 p.m. and the parade at 6 p.m. This year's theme is “America250: A Christmas Celebration.” The route begins at Mount Pleasant Road and Battlefield Boulevard and ends in front of City Hall on Cedar Road, and the parade is free.

Roads along the route close to all traffic from about 4 p.m. to 9 p.m. Parking is listed at the Municipal Complex behind the Central Library on Cedar Road, and the Great Bridge Community Center at 212 Holt Drive is listed as open during the parade. Hampton Roads Transit says Route 14 will not serve Cedar Road between Battlefield Boulevard and Albemarle Drive during those hours. Chris Jenkins's page on Great Bridge gives local background on that part of Chesapeake.

How Can You Use Holiday Events to Get to Know Hampton Roads?

If you are relocating or just beginning a home search, these evenings are a practical way to see how different parts of the region work on a busy night. Chris Jenkins suggests this simple routine:

  1. Time the drive. Notice how long it takes to reach an event from a neighborhood you are considering, and compare it with a weekday commute. Our page on how to test your commute in Hampton Roads explains how.
  2. Note parking and transit. Event nights show how roads, garages and light rail behave under heavy use.
  3. Visit more than once. Return in daylight on a quiet weekday to see the same area without the crowds.
  4. Keep a list. Write down what you liked about the home and its surroundings, then compare options with our neighborhood compare tool.
