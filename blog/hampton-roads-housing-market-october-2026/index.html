Hampton Roads Housing Market Update: October 2026 Numbers

Hampton Roads' October 2026 median price rose 2.26% to $370,180 as listings hit a six-year high. See inventory, days on market and rates for buyers and sellers.

By Chris Jenkins · Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group · Published November 26, 2026

Home/Blog/Hampton Roads Housing Market Update: October 2026 Numbers
Quick answer

In October 2026, REIN reported a Hampton Roads median sales price of $370,180, up 2.26% from a year earlier, with 6,603 active listings, the most since April 2020, and a median of 37 days on market. Sales slowed as 30-year mortgage rates averaged 7.28% in Freddie Mac's November 19 survey. Chris Jenkins of Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group helps buyers and sellers read what these numbers mean for their own home.

Short answer: Hampton Roads buyers have more homes to choose from than at any point in more than six years, while prices are still a little higher than last year. In its October 2026 report, REIN, the region's multiple listing service, put the median sales price at $370,180, up 2.26% from October 2025, with 6,603 active listings, up 18.5%. Sales slowed, homes took longer to sell, and mortgage rates were higher than a year ago. Chris Jenkins, a REALTOR® with Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group, breaks down what those numbers mean if you plan to buy or sell this winter.

What Did Hampton Roads Home Prices Do in October 2026?

Prices held up. REIN reported a regional median sales price of $370,180 for October, compared with $362,000 in October 2025, a 2.26% year-over-year gain. That is modest growth, not a jump, and REIN described prices as having leveled off somewhat compared with last year.

For context, REIN's September report showed a $375,000 median, up 2.74% from September 2025, and an August median of $388,950. Monthly medians move around with the mix of homes that close, so Chris Jenkins suggests watching the year-over-year change rather than reading too much into one month.

How Many Homes Are for Sale in Hampton Roads Right Now?

Inventory is the biggest story. Here are REIN's October 2026 figures for the region:

  • Active residential listings: 6,603, up from 6,357 in September and up 18.5% from 5,571 in October 2025. REIN says that is the highest count since April 2020.
  • Months supply of inventory: 3.11, up from 2.68 a year earlier and the highest since October 2019, according to REIN. On the Southside it was 2.96; on the Peninsula, 3.33.
  • Pending sales: 1,704, down 16.7% from 2,045 a year earlier.
  • Settled (closed) sales: 1,899, down 9.5% from 2,098 in October 2025.
  • New construction sold through the MLS: 190, compared with 187 a year earlier.

The same trend shows up statewide. Virginia Business, reporting Virginia REALTORS data, said Virginia had 28,560 active listings in August 2026, up 13.8% from a year earlier and the largest August supply since 2019.

How Long Are Homes Taking to Sell in Hampton Roads?

Longer than last year. REIN put the median days on market at 37 in October, compared with 30 in both September 2026 and October 2025. Southside homes had a median of 34 days, while Peninsula homes had a median of 40 days.

REIN's board president noted that the numbers often dip somewhat as the weather cools, so some of the slowdown is seasonal. Still, more listings and fewer pending sales mean buyers are not under the same pressure they felt in recent years. For city-level timing, see how long it takes to sell a house in Chesapeake and how long it takes to sell in Virginia Beach.

How Do Chesapeake, Virginia Beach and Norfolk Compare?

REIN's October release did not break out individual cities, but its September 2026 report listed year-over-year median price changes for the seven major cities:

  • Virginia Beach: up 8.4%, the largest gain among the seven
  • Chesapeake: up 2.5%
  • Norfolk: up 1.85%
  • Portsmouth and Hampton: each up 1.7%
  • Newport News: up 1.63%
  • Suffolk: down 3.26%, the only decline among the seven

City medians hide big differences between neighborhoods. To see current figures, read the median home price in Chesapeake or the median home price in Virginia Beach, or line up specific areas side by side with the neighborhood compare tool.

What Do Higher Mortgage Rates Mean for Affordability?

Rates matter as much as price. Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey reported a 30-year fixed average of 7.28% for the week of November 19, 2026, compared with 6.26% a year earlier. The 15-year fixed averaged 6.66%. Weekly averages change, so check the current figure and get a personal quote from a lender.

A higher rate raises the monthly payment on the same price, which is one reason pending sales fell. If you are using a VA loan, run your own numbers in the VA loan payment calculator and read about the 2026 VA funding fee before you set a price range.

What Does This Market Mean for Buyers?

More choice usually means more room to negotiate, though every home and seller is different. Chris Jenkins suggests a few steps for buyers this winter:

  1. Get pre-approved at today's rate, and ask your lender how the payment changes if rates move up or down.
  2. Look at days on market for each home you like; a listing that has sat longer may leave more room to discuss price, repairs or closing costs.
  3. Keep your inspection contingency. See the Virginia home inspection guide for what to expect.
  4. Compare neighborhoods on price, commute and costs like flood insurance and HOA dues, not just list price.

What Does This Market Mean for Sellers?

Sellers are competing with more listings, so pricing and presentation count. Overpricing in a market with three months of supply can mean a longer wait and a price cut later. Start with how to price your home to sell in Hampton Roads, and weigh timing with whether to sell in winter or spring. Prices are still above last year's levels, which is a solid starting point for many owners. None of this is a forecast; markets can shift quickly.

Common Questions

Quick Answers

Are home prices going down in Hampton Roads?
Not as of October 2026. REIN reported a regional median sales price of $370,180, up 2.26% from October 2025, though growth has leveled off. Chris Jenkins notes that prices vary by city and neighborhood, and Suffolk's September median was down 3.26% year over year.
Is it a buyer's market in Hampton Roads?
Conditions have shifted toward buyers. REIN reported 3.11 months of inventory in October 2026, the highest since October 2019, and the most active listings since April 2020. Homes took a median of 37 days to sell, up from 30 a year earlier.
How long does it take to sell a house in Hampton Roads right now?
REIN's median days on market was 37 in October 2026: 34 days on the Southside and 40 days on the Peninsula. Pricing, condition and location all change how long a specific home takes.
What are mortgage rates in Hampton Roads right now?
Freddie Mac's national survey averaged 7.28% for a 30-year fixed loan for the week of November 19, 2026, up from 6.26% a year earlier. Your own rate depends on your loan type, credit and lender, so get a personal quote.
Should I wait until spring to sell my Hampton Roads home?
It depends on your timeline and your home. Winter brings fewer sales but also fewer competing listings in some segments. Chris Jenkins of Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group can review your situation, with no promise about future prices.
