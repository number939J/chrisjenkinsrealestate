Virginia Beach Real Estate Tax Rate and Assessment Appeals

Virginia Beach's FY2027 real estate tax rate is $0.97 per $100. Learn how assessments change your bill, special districts, and how to appeal an assessment.

Short answer: Virginia Beach's real estate tax rate for fiscal year 2027 is $0.97 per $100 of assessed value, according to the city assessor's tax rates page. The rate did not go down, but home assessments rose, so many owners will see a higher bill. Chris Jenkins, a REALTOR® with Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group, walks buyers through this number before they write an offer, because it belongs in the monthly budget next to principal, interest and insurance.

What Is the Virginia Beach Real Estate Tax Rate for FY2027?

The assessor's tax rates page lists the general rate at $0.97 per $100 of assessed value for FY2027, and says real estate is assessed at 100% of fair market value. Virginia Beach City Council approved a budget of nearly $2.9 billion on May 13, 2026, and WTKR reported that it kept the rate at 97 cents. The fiscal year runs from July 1, so this rate is the one currently in effect as of November 18, 2026. Rates can change with each budget, so always confirm the current figure with the city.

To see what that means, here is a simple illustration (not a quote for any home): a $400,000 assessment at $0.97 per $100 works out to $3,880 a year, or about $323 a month. Your actual bill depends on the assessed value of the specific property, any special district, and any credits.

Why Can Your Bill Rise When the Rate Stays the Same?

Because the bill is the rate multiplied by the assessment. The city assessor's annual report says the median change in assessed value for all properties is 5.23%, and WTKR reported that home assessments rose an average of about 6%. WTKR gave an example in which a $400,000 assessment rising 6% to $424,000 would mean about $232.80 more at the current rate.

The city also cited some relief in the same budget. WTKR reported up to $80 off personal property tax per vehicle and a $77 real estate tax credit for homeowners. Treat those numbers as a starting point and check the City of Virginia Beach for eligibility and how the credit appears on your bill.

Do Some Neighborhoods Pay an Extra Special Service District Rate?

Yes, some areas have a special service district (SSD), which is an additional tax area that pays for specific improvements. The assessor's tax rates page lists SSD rates per $100 of assessed value, including Sandbridge at $0.98 and Shadowlawn at $1.1294, among others. Ask the city or a title professional whether a district applies to a particular address and exactly how it is billed alongside the general rate.

Chris Jenkins suggests asking for the full tax picture on any listing, not just the number printed on the flyer. You can read about Sandbridge Beach and Shadowlawn in our neighborhood guides, then verify the district details with the city for each address.

How Do You Appeal a Virginia Beach Assessment?

The assessor's website describes a two-stage process. Here is the order of steps:

  1. Watch for your notice. Reassessment notices are mailed by March 1 each year, and the city said the FY2027 notices were mailed February 27.
  2. Request a review hearing. The assessor's office holds hearings on request from March through April 30. Call (757) 385-4601 or email Assessor@vbgov.com.
  3. Gather evidence. The city explains that it values property by comparing it with recent sales of similar properties, so recent comparable sales and facts about your home's condition are what matter.
  4. Go to the Board of Equalization if needed. The assessor's site says the board hears cases from completed applications submitted by June 30. The board's phone is (757) 385-6296.

Deadlines can change, so confirm them on the assessor's site. Our Virginia Beach median home price page is a quick place to start when you want a sense of price levels, though an appeal turns on your own property's facts.

How Should Buyers Budget for Property Tax?

When you buy, the assessment may not match your purchase price, and a later reassessment can move it. That is why Chris Jenkins tells buyers to budget with some cushion rather than assume the seller's current bill will stay put. Add taxes to your housing payment early, and if you are using a VA loan, run the whole number through our VA loan payment calculator. If you are choosing between areas, the neighborhood compare tool helps you weigh price and housing type, and our flood insurance guide covers another cost to check before you commit.

Chesapeake, Norfolk and the other Hampton Roads cities set their own rates each year. This article covers only Virginia Beach, so look up the current rate for any other city before comparing totals.

Common Questions

Quick Answers

What is the real estate tax rate in Virginia Beach?
The City of Virginia Beach assessor lists $0.97 per $100 of assessed value for FY2027. Chris Jenkins recommends confirming the current rate with the city, since rates are set in each budget.
Why is my Virginia Beach property tax going up if the rate did not change?
The bill is the rate times the assessment. The city reports a median assessment increase of 5.23%, and WTKR reported an average of about 6% for homes.
How do I appeal my Virginia Beach home assessment?
Request a review hearing with the assessor from March through April 30, then apply to the Board of Equalization by June 30 if you disagree. Confirm dates with the city.
Do any Virginia Beach areas have an extra tax district?
Yes. The assessor lists special service district rates, such as Sandbridge at $0.98 and Shadowlawn at $1.1294 per $100. Ask the city how a district applies to a specific address.
