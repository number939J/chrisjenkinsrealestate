Selling Your Newport News House for Cash

Newport News runs along the James River on the Peninsula, with older neighborhoods near the shipyard and downtown, and newer subdivisions farther north. Values change sharply from one area to the next, so a cash offer has to be built from the closed sales around your address, not a citywide number.

I have not published Newport News neighborhood sales statistics on this site yet, so I will not quote a median here. When you send your address I pull nearby closed sales from REIN MLS, look at the condition of the house and the work it needs, and give you the numbers behind the offer.

I can also tell you what listing would likely bring. Read how to price a home to sell in Hampton Roads to see how I think about it.

Inherited Houses and Probate in Newport News

Virginia probate is handled by the circuit court clerk in the city or county where the person lived. For a Newport News resident that is the Newport News Circuit Court clerk. Read how to sell an inherited house in Hampton Roads and talk with a Virginia attorney about your estate.

Cash Sale vs. Listing With an Agent in Newport News

PriceA cash offer usually comes in below what a repaired, well-marketed home can fetch on the open market. You are paying for speed, certainty and convenience.
SpeedA cash buyer does not wait on mortgage approval or a lender appraisal. A listing depends on how fast the right buyer and their financing line up.
Repairs and showingsSell as-is with no repairs, staging or open houses, versus preparing and showing the home when you list.
CertaintyFewer contingencies means fewer ways for the deal to fall through, though every sale still depends on clear title and a signed contract.
CostsNo listing commission on a direct sale, but closing costs and taxes still apply. Virginia’s state recordation tax is 25 cents per $100 of the price (Va. Code § 58.1-801), and other fees can apply. Your settlement agent itemizes all of it.

If your home is in good shape and you are not in a hurry, listing with an agent will often net you more, and I will tell you that straight. I am a full-time REALTOR®, so I can help you either way.

Common Questions

Newport News Cash Sale Questions, Answered

Do you buy Newport News houses in any condition?
I look at houses in many conditions, including ones that need major work. Tell me what you know about the roof, systems, water or structural issues and I will give you an honest answer.
How do you decide what to offer on a Newport News house?
I pull closed sales near your address from REIN MLS, subtract the repairs and updates the house needs and the cost and risk of holding and reselling it, and show you the math. A cash offer is generally below what a fully prepared house could get on the open market.
I am stationed at Fort Eustis or Langley and have orders. How fast can this move?
As fast as clear title and your paperwork allow. Give me your report date and what is owed, and I will tell you honestly whether it works.
Can I sell my Newport News rental with a tenant in it?
Often, yes. Tenants have rights under their lease and Virginia law, so I will review the lease with you and, where needed, your attorney.
Who can sell an inherited Newport News house?
Generally the executor or administrator, after qualifying with the circuit court clerk. A Virginia attorney can confirm the steps for your estate.
Can I sell before foreclosure?
In most cases you can sell until the foreclosure sale takes place, with the proceeds paying off the lender. Read this guide and talk with a Virginia attorney or housing counselor.
Do I pay a Realtor commission if I sell to you in Newport News?
On a direct sale there is no listing commission. Closing costs and taxes still apply, and I will go over them with you before you decide. I will also tell you honestly what listing would likely net you.
How do I know this is not a scam?
Ask who the actual buyer is, ask for proof of funds, get the offer in writing, close through a title company or attorney and verify any license with the Virginia DPOR. I am a licensed Virginia REALTOR® and will tell you in writing when I am the buyer.
