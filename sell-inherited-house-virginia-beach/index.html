How to Sell an Inherited House in Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads

Inheriting a house can mean costs and decisions at a hard time. Here is how probate and sale work in Virginia, in plain language.

The short answer

In Virginia, probate is handled by the circuit court clerk where the person lived, and generally only a qualified executor or administrator can sell. This is general information, not legal advice. Chris Jenkins can buy for cash or list.

Step 1: Find Who Has Authority to Sign

Probate is handled by the circuit court clerk in the city or county where the person lived. Under Virginia law an executor named in a will, or an administrator the court appoints, typically has authority only after qualifying. See also Virginia Cooperative Extension’s guide to settling an estate.

A personal representative’s power to sell real estate is addressed in Va. Code § 64.2-521. Ask a Virginia attorney what applies to your estate.

Step 2: Decide Keep, List or Sell for Cash

  • Keep or rent: best if you want the asset and can cover taxes and upkeep.
  • List: usually the highest price, but you must prepare the house.
  • Sell for cash as-is: faster and no repairs, usually at a lower price.

Heirs often disagree, so settle this early, in writing.

Step 3: Costs While You Decide

Taxes, insurance, utilities and upkeep continue. Recordation tax and other transfer costs fall under the Virginia Recordation Tax Act; your closing agent will itemize them.

Cash Sale vs. Listing With an Agent in Hampton Roads

PriceA cash offer usually comes in below what a repaired, well-marketed home can fetch on the open market. You are paying for speed, certainty and convenience.
SpeedA cash buyer does not wait on mortgage approval or a lender appraisal. A listing depends on how fast the right buyer and their financing line up.
Repairs and showingsSell as-is with no repairs, staging or open houses, versus preparing and showing the home when you list.
CertaintyFewer contingencies means fewer ways for the deal to fall through, though every sale still depends on clear title and a signed contract.
CostsNo listing commission on a direct sale, but closing costs and taxes still apply. Virginia’s state recordation tax is 25 cents per $100 of the price (Va. Code § 58.1-801), and other fees can apply. Your settlement agent itemizes all of it.

If your home is in good shape and you are not in a hurry, listing with an agent will often net you more, and I will tell you that straight. I am a full-time REALTOR®, so I can help you either way.

Hampton Roads Cash Sale Questions, Answered

Can I sell an inherited house before probate ends?
Often only after the personal representative qualifies. Ask a Virginia attorney.
Do all heirs have to agree?
It depends on the will and how title passes. With several heirs, get legal advice before listing or accepting any offer.
What if the house needs lots of work?
A direct as-is sale avoids repairs and showings. Compare it against listing.
