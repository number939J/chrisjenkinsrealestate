Selling Your Virginia Beach House for Cash: What to Expect

Virginia Beach is too big to treat as one market. REIN MLS splits it into eight market areas, and the price gap between neighborhoods is enormous. REIN records show 12,010 closed residential sales in Virginia Beach between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026. The citywide median sold price was $405,000, the median home took 15 days to sell, and the median price per square foot was $240.

Inside that average, the median in Campus East was $250,000 (0.6x the citywide figure), while in Ashville Park it was $1,218,153 (3.0x). That is why I do not give a cash offer from a citywide number. I look at the sold prices closest to your address, the condition of your house and the work it needs, and then show you how I got to the offer.

If you would like the full local picture first, the Virginia Beach neighborhood guides show the numbers for each community, and this short answer covers the citywide median.

Sell My House Fast in Virginia Beach: Military Timelines

Virginia Beach is home to Naval Air Station Oceana, Naval Amphibious Base Little Creek and the NAVSEA Dam Neck annex, and many Virginia Beach sellers are on a Navy or joint-service clock. A report date is a hard deadline. Listing a house takes time, and carrying two housing payments while you wait for a buyer’s financing is expensive.

In that situation a direct sale can take the uncertainty out of timing. It is not always the right move, especially if you may want to keep the house as a rental or you have time to list, so read the guide to VA loan entitlement and PCS moves first. If your home is near the air station, also read about military noise zone disclosure, since that can affect how buyers see the property.

Selling a Virginia Beach Rental: The Certificate of Compliance

If you own a rental in a designated area, the city runs a Certificate of Compliance rental inspection program. It established Rental Inspection Districts to prevent deterioration and blight, owners must register applicable properties with Code Enforcement, and the city charges a $50 fee for the inspection of each rental dwelling unit in a district.

That requirement is one reason landlords decide to sell. If you are tired of managing a rental, dealing with inspections or carrying a vacant unit, tell me about the lease, the rent and the condition of the property, and we can talk through a sale, with or without the tenant in place.

Coastal Homes, Flood Zones and What You Must Disclose

Selling as-is does not erase Virginia’s disclosure rules. Owners of residential property generally must give the buyer a residential property disclosure statement, which was updated effective July 1, 2025. Separately, an owner who has actual knowledge that a dwelling is a repetitive risk loss structure must tell the purchaser.

Because so many Virginia Beach homes are near water, it is smart to know your flood zone before you sell. Read why elevation certificates matter and whether you need flood insurance.

Cash Sale vs. Listing With an Agent in Virginia Beach

PriceA cash offer usually comes in below what a repaired, well-marketed home can fetch on the open market. You are paying for speed, certainty and convenience.
SpeedA cash buyer does not wait on mortgage approval or a lender appraisal. A listing depends on how fast the right buyer and their financing line up.
Repairs and showingsSell as-is with no repairs, staging or open houses, versus preparing and showing the home when you list.
CertaintyFewer contingencies means fewer ways for the deal to fall through, though every sale still depends on clear title and a signed contract.
CostsNo listing commission on a direct sale, but closing costs and taxes still apply. Virginia’s state recordation tax is 25 cents per $100 of the price (Va. Code § 58.1-801), and other fees can apply. Your settlement agent itemizes all of it.

If your home is in good shape and you are not in a hurry, listing with an agent will often net you more, and I will tell you that straight. I am a full-time REALTOR®, so I can help you either way.

Virginia Beach Cash Sale Questions, Answered

Do you buy Virginia Beach houses in any condition?
I look at houses in many conditions, including ones that need major repairs. Tell me about the roof, systems, water or foundation issues and anything you know is wrong, and I will give you an honest answer on whether it is something I can take on.
How is a Virginia Beach cash offer calculated?
I start from what similar nearby homes have sold for, then subtract the cost of repairs and updates and the cost and risk of holding and reselling the house. That is why a cash offer is generally below what a fully prepared home could get on the open market, and why it varies so much by neighborhood. I will show you the math.
Do you buy higher-priced homes in places like Sandbridge or the North End?
I am happy to look at any Virginia Beach address. For well-kept homes in high-demand areas, though, listing frequently makes more sense because the buyer pool is large. See the numbers for Sandbridge and the North End and I will give you an honest comparison.
I own a rental in a Rental Inspection District. Can I still sell?
Yes. Owners in a district register with Code Enforcement and pay a $50 inspection fee per unit under the city’s Certificate of Compliance program. Tell me about the property and the tenant situation and we can figure out the cleanest way to sell.
I have PCS orders. How fast can this happen?
As fast as clear title and your own paperwork allow. Tell me your report date and what is still owed on the house, and I will say honestly whether a direct sale or a listing fits your timeline.
How long would it take to sell if I listed instead?
In the last 24 months the median Virginia Beach home took 15 days to sell, according to REIN. That is for the typical house, not necessarily yours. Condition and price matter a lot. Read days to sell a house in Virginia Beach.
Do I pay a Realtor commission if I sell to you?
On a direct sale there is no listing commission. Closing costs and taxes still apply, and I will go over them with you before you decide. I will also give you my honest view of what listing would likely net you.
