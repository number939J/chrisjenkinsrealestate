Estimate your monthly payment on a VA loan in Chesapeake, Virginia Beach and the rest of Hampton Roads, with the funding fee, property tax, insurance and your BAH. Built by Chris Jenkins.

Home/VA Loan Payment Calculator
What this does

Enter a price, down payment and your lender’s rate to see an estimated monthly payment including the VA funding fee, city property tax, insurance and flood insurance, and how it compares with your BAH. The interest rate, insurance and BAH fields are yours to fill in; nothing here is a rate quote.

How This Is Calculated

  • Funding fee: the published VA purchase-loan chart: first use 2.15% (under 5% down), 1.5% (5% to under 10%), 1.25% (10% or more); subsequent use 3.3% under 5% down, then the same 1.5% and 1.25%. Exempt borrowers pay none.
  • Property tax: price times the city rate per $100, divided by 12. Virginia Beach’s general rate is $0.97 per $100 per the city assessor, and Chesapeake is set to $1.01 per $100, the rate Virginia Beach’s city blog cited in February 2025. Confirm the current rate with the city, and note special service districts can add charges.
  • Principal and interest: a standard fixed-rate payment for the term you choose.

For flood zones, HOA packets and military noise zones, use our Hampton Roads buying checklist, and see whether you need flood insurance. Compare areas with the neighborhood compare tool. This is an estimate for planning, not a loan offer or financial advice; ask a VA-approved lender for exact figures.

Common Questions

About the VA Loan Calculator

What is the VA funding fee?
It is a one-time fee on VA purchase loans. For a first use it is 2.15% with less than 5% down, 1.5% with 5% to under 10% down, and 1.25% with 10% or more down. For subsequent use with less than 5% down it is 3.3%. Veterans receiving VA disability compensation and certain surviving spouses are exempt, so confirm your status with your lender.
Can I roll the VA funding fee into the loan?
Yes, the funding fee can generally be financed, which raises the loan amount. This calculator lets you choose either way so you can see the effect on the monthly payment and on cash at closing.
Is the property tax figure exact?
No. It multiplies the purchase price by the city's rate per $100 of assessed value, which is a simplification. Your actual bill uses the city's assessed value and may include special district charges, so check the specific property.
Does this include flood insurance?
Only if you enter it. Flood insurance varies by address in Hampton Roads, so get a quote for the specific home and enter the yearly premium.
How should I use the BAH field?
Enter your monthly Basic Allowance for Housing from your official pay information. The calculator then shows your estimated payment as a multiple of your BAH. Chris Jenkins can help you compare homes against that number.
