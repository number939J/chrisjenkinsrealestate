Common Questions

About the Compare Tool

What does the neighborhood compare tool show?
It puts two Chesapeake or Virginia Beach neighborhoods side by side using REIN MLS closed sales: median sold price, price per square foot, days on market, home size, year built and sale-to-list ratio, with multiples showing how far apart they are.
Where does the data come from?
REIN MLS closed residential sales between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026. Medians are used so a few unusual sales do not skew the numbers, and neighborhoods follow MLS subdivision names.
Is the tool free to use or embed?
Yes. It is free, and you can embed it on your own site with the code on this page. The embed links back to Chris Jenkins at Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate – Native American Group.
