Bryans Cove Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 79 residential sales closed in Bryans Cove (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $424,900, close to the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 28 days to sell, slower than the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Bryans Cove sales closed between $397,090 and $439,950, or about $201 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($434,000, 45 sales) was about 3% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($419,950, 34 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Bryans Cove sales were in ZIP code 23323.

What Stands Out About Bryans Cove

100% of the homes sold here were built in 2015 or later; across Chesapeake the figure is 24%. 0% of sales here were detached homes, versus 76% citywide. 9% of Bryans Cove sales had four or more bedrooms, against 53% citywide.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Bryans Cove

All of the sales were attached homes such as townhomes or condos. The median home was about 2,036 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,915 to 2,432 square feet. 91% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 2019 and 2023, with a median year built of 2020, so this is mostly newer construction.
