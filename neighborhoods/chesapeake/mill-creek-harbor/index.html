Where Mill Creek Harbor Is in Chesapeake

Mill Creek Harbor sits in the Grassfield section of South Chesapeake, Virginia. Real estate listings place it in MLS Area 32 (South Chesapeake) with a 23323 ZIP code. Listing descriptions routinely describe it as a well-established community and mention convenient access to shopping, boat ramps and the interstate.

One thing that trips up online searches: Mill Creek Harbor is not the same as Mill Creek Parkway or Culpepper Landing. Those are separate communities nearby with their own associations, amenities and school assignments. Another nearby option is The Cove on New Mill Creek, a small new-construction community of waterfront and water-view lots by Stephen Alexander Homes, where builder-listed base pricing started in the high $700,000s on new-home portals when this guide was researched. If you are comparing communities, Chris Jenkins can line them up side by side for you.

Homes You'll Find in Mill Creek Harbor

Homes here are primarily detached single-family houses. The listings reviewed for this guide include 4-bedroom homes built in 2003 with about 2,850 to 3,000 square feet, on lots around a quarter acre or larger, many with attached two-car garages. Some properties have in-ground pools, some sit on cul-de-sacs, and some are on the water. Others are not waterfront, and at least one listing notes a double gate that makes room for an RV, boat or trailer.

Because every lot is different, the most useful comparison is always against recent sales of similar homes on the same or nearby streets. Chris Jenkins pulls those comparable sales from the MLS for you instead of relying on a neighborhood average.

HOA Dues, Flood Zones and Other Costs to Check

Mill Creek Harbor has a homeowners association. Listing records for homes here show monthly dues in roughly the $25 to $32 range, which is on the modest side, but dues change and you should confirm the current amount and what it covers before you write an offer. If you buy, make sure you receive and read the association disclosure packet. If you sell, Chris Jenkins will help you get yours ordered on time.

Waterfront and low-lying lots deserve a flood-zone check. Listings in the community differ: one recent listing advertises that flood insurance is not required, while waterfront homes elsewhere in the neighborhood may be treated differently. Do not assume. Ask for the FEMA flood-zone determination and an insurance quote early in the process. Chris Jenkins builds that step into every Mill Creek Harbor purchase.

Virginia is also a disclosure-statement state: sellers give buyers a Residential Property Disclosure Statement that tells the buyer to do their own due diligence. That makes a thorough home inspection essential. Read more in our guide to the Virginia Residential Property Disclosure Statement.

Schools and Commute Questions

Listings for Mill Creek Harbor homes show the zoned schools as Grassfield Elementary, Hugo A. Owens Middle and Grassfield High. School zones can be redrawn, so confirm the current assignment with Chesapeake Public Schools before relying on it.

If you are relocating for work or military orders, the practical question is drive time. Ask Chris Jenkins to run realistic commute checks from Mill Creek Harbor to Naval Station Norfolk, NAS Oceana, JEB Little Creek, Langley AFB, the shipyards or your office, at the hours you will actually drive. If you are near a military air installation flight area, also review the Navy's noise and accident-potential maps for any home you consider.
