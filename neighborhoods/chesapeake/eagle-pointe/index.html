Eagle Pointe Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 51 residential sales closed in Eagle Pointe (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $470,000, close to the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 4 days to sell, faster than the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Eagle Pointe sales closed between $429,450 and $528,938, or about $223 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($517,500, 26 sales) was about 12% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($462,503, 25 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Eagle Pointe sales were in ZIP code 23322.

What Stands Out About Eagle Pointe

10% of Eagle Pointe sales had four or more bedrooms, against 53% citywide. 22% of sales here were detached homes, versus 76% citywide. 65% of Eagle Pointe homes went under contract within 14 days of listing, compared with 44% of Chesapeake sales overall.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Eagle Pointe

22% of the sales were detached homes and 78% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 2,130 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,870 to 2,426 square feet. 90% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 2006 and 2017, with a median year built of 2012, so this is a mix of homes from the 1990s to the 2010s.
