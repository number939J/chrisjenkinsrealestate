How Chesapeake Is Laid Out

Chesapeake is not one single market. Home shoppers usually think in terms of its sections: Great Bridge in the central part of the city, Deep Creek and the Grassfield area to the south, Greenbrier around the Battlefield Boulevard and Greenbrier Parkway corridors, Western Branch toward Portsmouth and Suffolk, and Indian River and South Norfolk closer to Norfolk. Each has its own mix of home ages, lot sizes, association fees and commute patterns, which is why a neighborhood-level guide matters more than a citywide average.

Chris Jenkins has represented buyers and sellers in many of these areas, including Greenbrier East, Great Bridge and Indian River, according to client reviews on his reviews section.
