Hickory Manor Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 87 residential sales closed in Hickory Manor (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $590,000, above the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 30 days to sell, slower than the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Hickory Manor sales closed between $486,750 and $625,000, or about $242 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($575,000, 41 sales) was about 6% lower than in the earlier 12 months ($609,900, 46 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Hickory Manor

74% of the sales were detached homes and 26% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 2,456 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 2,068 to 2,524 square feet. 49% of the homes that sold had 4 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 2019 and 2024, with a median year built of 2021, so this is mostly newer construction.

About These Hickory Manor Numbers

Figures come from closed residential sales recorded in REIN MLS for Chesapeake between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026, grouped under the subdivision name MLS agents use. Medians, not averages, are used so a few very high or low sales do not distort the picture. With 87 sales, this is a solid sample, but every home differs and these figures are not an appraisal or a prediction. Subdivision names in MLS data can cover more than one section of a community.
