Jolliff Landing at Western Branch Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 119 residential sales closed in Jolliff Landing at Western Branch (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $554,900, above the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 33 days to sell, slower than the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Jolliff Landing at Western Branch sales closed between $540,900 and $577,396, or about $225 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($559,385, 60 sales) was about 1% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($554,165, 59 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

What Stands Out About Jolliff Landing at Western Branch

100% of the homes sold here were built in 2015 or later; across Chesapeake the figure is 24%. 100% of Jolliff Landing at Western Branch sales had four or more bedrooms, against 53% citywide. The median Jolliff Landing at Western Branch home is about 2,454 square feet, larger than the 1,970 square foot citywide median.
