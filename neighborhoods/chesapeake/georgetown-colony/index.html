Georgetown Colony Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 52 residential sales closed in Georgetown Colony (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $270,700, below the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 12 days to sell, faster than the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Georgetown Colony sales closed between $255,000 and $373,000, or about $193 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($282,000, 28 sales) was about 8% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($260,000, 24 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Georgetown Colony sales were in ZIP code 23325.

What Stands Out About Georgetown Colony

31% of sales here were detached homes, versus 76% citywide. 25% of Georgetown Colony sales had four or more bedrooms, against 53% citywide. 0% of the homes sold here were built in 2015 or later; across Chesapeake the figure is 24%.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Georgetown Colony

31% of the sales were detached homes and 69% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,377 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,260 to 1,951 square feet. 44% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 1972 and 1987, with a median year built of 1985, so this is mostly homes from the 1970s through the 1990s.
