Portlock Terrace Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 58 residential sales closed in Portlock Terrace (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $319,250, below the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 18 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Portlock Terrace sales closed between $281,175 and $349,999, or about $204 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($319,000, 29 sales) was about 2% lower than in the earlier 12 months ($325,000, 29 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Portlock Terrace sales were in ZIP code 23324.

What Stands Out About Portlock Terrace

33% of Portlock Terrace sales had four or more bedrooms, against 53% citywide. 100% of sales here were detached homes, versus 76% citywide. The median Portlock Terrace home is about 1,498 square feet, smaller than the 1,970 square foot citywide median.
