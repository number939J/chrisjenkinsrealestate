Western Branch Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 116 residential sales closed in Western Branch (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $355,000, close to the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 20 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Western Branch sales closed between $255,000 and $445,125, or about $201 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($345,000, 57 sales) was about 4% lower than in the earlier 12 months ($360,000, 59 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Western Branch sales were in ZIP code 23321.

What Stands Out About Western Branch

9% of the homes sold here were built in 2015 or later; across Chesapeake the figure is 24%. 39% of Western Branch sales had four or more bedrooms, against 53% citywide. The median Western Branch home is about 1,703 square feet, smaller than the 1,970 square foot citywide median.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Western Branch

66% of the sales were detached homes and 34% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,703 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,316 to 2,201 square feet. 50% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Home ages vary widely here: about 80% of sales were built between 1964 and 2013, with a median year built of 2000.
