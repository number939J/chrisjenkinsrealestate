Camelot Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 89 residential sales closed in Camelot (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $337,500, close to the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 14 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Camelot sales closed between $299,900 and $364,900, or about $194 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($345,000, 45 sales) was about 5% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($330,000, 44 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Camelot sales were in ZIP code 23323.

What Stands Out About Camelot

1% of the homes sold here were built in 2015 or later; across Chesapeake the figure is 24%. The median Camelot home is about 1,695 square feet, smaller than the 1,970 square foot citywide median. 89% of sales here were detached homes, versus 76% citywide.

When Camelot Homes Sell and How Far Prices Range

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Camelot

89% of the sales were detached homes and 11% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,695 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,406 to 1,932 square feet. 47% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 1969 and 1987, with a median year built of 1972, so this is mostly homes from the 1970s through the 1990s.
