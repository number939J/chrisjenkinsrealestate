Hickory Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 122 residential sales closed in Hickory (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $717,500, well above the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 24 days to sell, slower than the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Hickory sales closed between $550,625 and $893,750, or about $256 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($750,000, 67 sales) was about 6% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($704,550, 55 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Hickory sales were in ZIP code 23322.

What Stands Out About Hickory

The median Hickory home is about 2,800 square feet, larger than the 1,970 square foot citywide median. 79% of Hickory sales had four or more bedrooms, against 53% citywide. 100% of sales here were detached homes, versus 76% citywide.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Hickory

All of the sales were detached single-family homes. The median home was about 2,800 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 2,113 to 3,374 square feet. 51% of the homes that sold had 4 bedrooms.

Home ages vary widely here: about 80% of sales were built between 1954 and 2021, with a median year built of 1998.
