Las Gaviotas Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 57 residential sales closed in Las Gaviotas (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $560,000, above the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 12 days to sell, faster than the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Las Gaviotas sales closed between $411,000 and $610,000, or about $220 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($575,000, 29 sales) was about 8% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($530,000, 28 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Las Gaviotas sales were in ZIP code 23322.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Las Gaviotas

77% of the sales were detached homes and 23% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 2,503 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 2,012 to 2,813 square feet. 61% of the homes that sold had 4 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 1985 and 1993, with a median year built of 1988, so this is mostly homes from the 1970s through the 1990s.
