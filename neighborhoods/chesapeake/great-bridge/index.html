Great Bridge Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 89 residential sales closed in Great Bridge (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $429,900, close to the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 16 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Great Bridge sales closed between $345,000 and $575,000, or about $242 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($465,000, 41 sales) was about 13% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($410,220, 48 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Great Bridge

84% of the sales were detached homes and 16% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,716 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,466 to 2,400 square feet. 38% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Home ages vary widely here: about 80% of sales were built between 1957 and 2021, with a median year built of 1995.

Averages hide a lot, so Chris Jenkins compares you to homes that match yours on street, size, age and condition rather than to the whole Great Bridge median.

About These Great Bridge Numbers

Figures come from closed residential sales recorded in REIN MLS for Chesapeake between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026, grouped under the subdivision name MLS agents use. Medians, not averages, are used so a few very high or low sales do not distort the picture. With 89 sales, this is a solid sample, but every home differs and these figures are not an appraisal or a prediction. Subdivision names in MLS data can cover more than one section of a community.
