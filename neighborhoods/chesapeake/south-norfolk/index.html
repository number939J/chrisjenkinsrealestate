South Norfolk Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 217 residential sales closed in South Norfolk (Chesapeake, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $297,000, below the citywide median of $420,000, and homes took a median of 23 days to sell, slower than the citywide median of 18 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of South Norfolk sales closed between $230,000 and $355,000, or about $188 per square foot against $219 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($302,450, 104 sales) was about 4% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($292,000, 113 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

What Kinds of Homes Sell in South Norfolk

95% of the sales were detached homes and 5% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,514 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,244 to 1,948 square feet. 48% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Home ages vary widely here: about 80% of sales were built between 1910 and 2021, with a median year built of 1956.

About These South Norfolk Numbers

Figures come from closed residential sales recorded in REIN MLS for Chesapeake between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026, grouped under the subdivision name MLS agents use. Medians, not averages, are used so a few very high or low sales do not distort the picture. With 217 sales, this is a solid sample, but every home differs and these figures are not an appraisal or a prediction. Subdivision names in MLS data can cover more than one section of a community.
