Ashville Park Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 109 residential sales closed in Ashville Park (Virginia Beach, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $1,218,153, well above the citywide median of $405,000, and homes took a median of 11 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 15 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Ashville Park sales closed between $949,900 and $1,325,126, or about $326 per square foot against $240 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($1,205,791, 44 sales) was about 1% lower than in the earlier 12 months ($1,220,313, 65 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Ashville Park sales were in ZIP code 23456.

What Stands Out About Ashville Park

The median Ashville Park home is about 3,669 square feet, larger than the 1,706 square foot citywide median. 81% of the homes sold here were built in 2015 or later; across Virginia Beach the figure is 7%. 100% of Ashville Park sales had four or more bedrooms, against 40% citywide.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Ashville Park

All of the sales were detached single-family homes. The median home was about 3,669 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 3,270 to 3,734 square feet. 50% of the homes that sold had 4 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 2013 and 2026, with a median year built of 2024, so this is mostly newer construction.
