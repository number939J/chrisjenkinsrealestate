Virginia Beach VA Neighborhoods & Real Estate Guide

Virginia Beach is an independent city on the Atlantic coast of southeastern Virginia and the most populous city in the state. Home/Neighborhoods/Virginia Beach, VA
At a glance

Virginia Beach, VA is a Hampton Roads city that REIN MLS divides into eight market areas (Areas 41–48), from Northwest Virginia Beach to Southwest 2. REIN records show 12,010 closed residential sales between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026, a median sold price of $405,000 and a median of 15 days on market. How the Virginia Beach Market Is Organized

Virginia Beach is too large to treat as one market. REIN MLS splits it into eight areas: Northwest (Area 41), North Central (42), Northeast (43), Southeast (44), South Central 1 (45), Southwest 1 (46), South Central 2 (47) and Southwest 2 (48). The busiest by sales volume in the last 24 months were Area 43 (Northeast), Area 41 (Northwest) and Area 47 (South Central 2). Within those areas, buyers shop by neighborhood and subdivision name, which is how the guides below are organized.

Virginia Beach Home Prices: What Sold in the Last 24 Months

REIN MLS records show 12,010 closed residential sales in Virginia Beach between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026. The citywide median sold price was $405,000, the median home took 15 days to sell, and the median price per square foot was $240. Neighborhoods differ enormously: the median sold price in Campus East was $250,000, while in Ashville Park it was $1,218,153. That gap is why each guide below shows the numbers for its own community instead of a citywide average.
