Aragona Village Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 234 residential sales closed in Aragona Village (Virginia Beach, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $345,000, close to the citywide median of $405,000, and homes took a median of 11 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 15 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Aragona Village sales closed between $310,250 and $370,000, or about $247 per square foot against $240 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($350,000, 102 sales) was about 4% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($335,000, 132 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Most recent Aragona Village sales were in ZIP code 23455, with a meaningful share in 23462.

What Stands Out About Aragona Village

99% of sales here were detached homes, versus 61% citywide. The median Aragona Village home is about 1,400 square feet, smaller than the 1,706 square foot citywide median. 60% of Aragona Village homes went under contract within 14 days of listing, compared with 49% of Virginia Beach sales overall.

When Aragona Village Homes Sell and How Far Prices Range

Closings in Aragona Village were busiest in August (30 sales across the two years) and slowest in January (11 sales). Sold prices ran from $154,000 to $950,000, a spread of 6.2x between the lowest and highest sale. 78% of homes closed at or above list price and 6% closed at least 5% below it.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Aragona Village

99% of the sales were detached homes and 1% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,400 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,202 to 1,512 square feet. 52% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 1956 and 1960, with a median year built of 1958, so this is mostly older homes.
