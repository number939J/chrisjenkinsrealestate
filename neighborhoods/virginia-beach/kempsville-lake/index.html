Kempsville Lake Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 138 residential sales closed in Kempsville Lake (Virginia Beach, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $315,000, below the citywide median of $405,000, and homes took a median of 13 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 15 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Kempsville Lake sales closed between $300,000 and $326,075, or about $202 per square foot against $240 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($319,900, 71 sales) was about 2% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($315,000, 67 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Kempsville Lake sales were in ZIP code 23462.

What Stands Out About Kempsville Lake

1% of sales here were detached homes, versus 61% citywide. 7% of Kempsville Lake sales had four or more bedrooms, against 40% citywide. At a median $202 per square foot, Kempsville Lake sits 16% below the $240 citywide figure.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Kempsville Lake

1% of the sales were detached homes and 99% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,607 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,424 to 1,668 square feet. 81% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 1982 and 1989, with a median year built of 1984, so this is mostly homes from the 1970s through the 1990s.
