Ridgely Manor Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 96 residential sales closed in Ridgely Manor (Virginia Beach, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $302,100, below the citywide median of $405,000, and homes took a median of 20 days to sell, slower than the citywide median of 15 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Ridgely Manor sales closed between $244,975 and $375,750, or about $198 per square foot against $240 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($309,750, 50 sales) was about 3% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($301,550, 46 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Ridgely Manor

17% of the sales were detached homes and 83% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,424 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,367 to 1,809 square feet. 58% of the homes that sold had 2 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 2006 and 2010, with a median year built of 2008, so this is a mix of homes from the 1990s to the 2010s.

About These Ridgely Manor Numbers

Figures come from closed residential sales recorded in REIN MLS for Virginia Beach between November 15, 2024 and November 14, 2026, grouped under the subdivision name MLS agents use. Medians, not averages, are used so a few very high or low sales do not distort the picture. With 96 sales, this is a solid sample, but every home differs and these figures are not an appraisal or a prediction. Subdivision names in MLS data can cover more than one section of a community.
