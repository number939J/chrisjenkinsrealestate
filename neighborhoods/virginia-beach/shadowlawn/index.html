Shadowlawn Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 107 residential sales closed in Shadowlawn (Virginia Beach, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $525,000, above the citywide median of $405,000, and homes took a median of 18 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 15 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Shadowlawn sales closed between $286,625 and $790,000, or about $341 per square foot against $240 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($486,000, 53 sales) was about 11% lower than in the earlier 12 months ($545,000, 54 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Shadowlawn sales were in ZIP code 23451.

What Stands Out About Shadowlawn

At a median $341 per square foot, Shadowlawn sits 42% above the $240 citywide figure. 26% of Shadowlawn sales had four or more bedrooms, against 40% citywide. 22% of Shadowlawn homes sold for more than their list price, versus 36% citywide, so sellers here more often accepted less than the asking price.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Shadowlawn

48% of the sales were detached homes and 52% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,510 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,017 to 2,180 square feet. 36% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Home ages vary widely here: about 80% of sales were built between 1966 and 2019, with a median year built of 1983.
