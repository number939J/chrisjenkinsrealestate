Ocean Lakes Home Prices and Market Pace

In the 24 months from November 15, 2024 to November 14, 2026, 287 residential sales closed in Ocean Lakes (Virginia Beach, VA) according to REIN MLS records. The median sold price was $420,000, close to the citywide median of $405,000, and homes took a median of 12 days to sell, about the same pace as the citywide median of 15 days. These figures come from closed sales, which show what homes actually sold for rather than asking prices.

The middle half of Ocean Lakes sales closed between $337,000 and $460,000, or about $256 per square foot against $240 citywide. Split by year, the median sold price in the most recent 12 months ($429,000, 146 sales) was about 5% higher than in the earlier 12 months ($408,000, 141 sales). The typical home sold right at its list price (median sale-to-list ratio of 100%).

Recent Ocean Lakes sales were in ZIP code 23454.

What Stands Out About Ocean Lakes

31% of Ocean Lakes sales had four or more bedrooms, against 40% citywide. The median Ocean Lakes home is about 1,541 square feet, smaller than the 1,706 square foot citywide median. 1% of the homes sold here were built in 2015 or later; across Virginia Beach the figure is 7%.

Closings in Ocean Lakes were busiest in May (47 sales across the two years) and slowest in January (14 sales). Sold prices ran from $177,300 to $660,000, a spread of 3.7x between the lowest and highest sale. 79% of homes closed at or above list price and 2% closed at least 5% below it.

What Kinds of Homes Sell in Ocean Lakes

61% of the sales were detached homes and 39% were attached homes (townhomes or condos). The median home was about 1,541 square feet, with the middle half of sales running from roughly 1,383 to 1,716 square feet. 55% of the homes that sold had 3 bedrooms.

Most homes that sold were built between 1986 and 1992, with a median year built of 1988, so this is mostly homes from the 1970s through the 1990s.
